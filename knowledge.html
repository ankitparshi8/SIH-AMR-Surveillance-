Pathogen & Antibiotic Knowledge Base

Medical reference for bacterial infections and recommended clinical drugs

Escherichia coli (E. coli)

Primary Infection: Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)

Common Symptoms: Burning sensation during urination, abdominal pain.

First-line Antibiotics: Ciprofloxacin, Nitrofurantoin, Ampicillin.

Resistance Risk: High against Ampicillin.

WHO Priority: High

Klebsiella pneumoniae

Primary Infection: Pneumonia & Bloodstream Infection

Common Symptoms: High fever, shortness of breath, chest pain.

First-line Antibiotics: Ceftriaxone, Meropenem, Gentamicin.

Resistance Risk: Severe against 3rd Gen Cephalosporins.

WHO Priority: Critical

Pseudomonas aeruginosa

Primary Infection: Hospital-acquired Wounds & Burn Infections

Common Symptoms: Blue-green pus formation, fever, localized swelling.

First-line Antibiotics: Piperacillin-Tazobactam, Amikacin.

Resistance Risk: Multi-drug resistant strains emerging.

WHO Priority: Critical

Staphylococcus aureus

Primary Infection: Skin Infections & Abscesses

Common Symptoms: Red bumps, boils, skin swelling with pain.

First-line Antibiotics: Oxacillin, Vancomycin, Daptomycin.

Resistance Risk: MRSA (Methicillin-Resistant S. aureus).

WHO Priority: High
