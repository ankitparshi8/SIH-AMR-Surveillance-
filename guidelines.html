WHO AWaRe Classification and Prescription Best Practices

🌍 WHO AWaRe Classification Framework

🟢 ACCESS Group

First and second-choice antibiotics for common infections. High therapeutic value and lower resistance potential.


Examples: Amoxicillin, Ampicillin, Doxycycline

🟠 WATCH Group

Antibiotics with higher resistance potential. Recommended only for specific, limited indications.


Examples: Ciprofloxacin, Ceftriaxone, Meropenem

🔴 RESERVE Group

"Last resort" options that should be reserved for confirmed multi-drug resistant infections.


Examples: Colistin, Linezolid

✅ Clinical Prescribing Checklist

  • ✔️ Confirm Bacterial Infection: Avoid prescribing antibiotics for viral upper respiratory infections.
  • ✔️ Obtain Culture Reports: Send lab cultures before initiating broad-spectrum empirical therapy.
  • ✔️ De-escalation Protocol: Switch from broad-spectrum to narrow-spectrum drugs once lab results arrive.
  • ✔️ Optimize Dosage: Ensure dose duration is strictly adhered to prevent incomplete course mutation risks.
